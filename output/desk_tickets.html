What the agent team should do with this ticket, from the data above.

Boss calls first

Facilities. A rent ticket is a different shape from an order: there is no SKU and no size, so there is nothing for Inventory to answer. What decides this ticket is the lease — and the lease is the record, not the email that raised it.

Workflow

  1. Boss → Facilities: what does lease #1 actually say? Chapel Street shop, Elm City Properties, $2,400.00, next due 2026-09-02 — 2 days from the shop's date. Not overdue.
  2. Facilities reports the lease row, not the notice. A notice can be wrong about the amount or the date; the lease cannot.
  3. Boss → Accounting: can it be paid? Cash is $3,400.00, so yes — on its own it leaves $1,000.00, or $160.00 if the overdue invoice is paid first.
  4. Accounting prepares it for human approval and stops there. Rent waits on a person like every other payment.
  5. Boss → Customer Service: draft to Elm City Properties acknowledging the due date. Saved to the board, never sent.
  6. Boss makes the final call: ticket stays open until the rent is approved and paid.
